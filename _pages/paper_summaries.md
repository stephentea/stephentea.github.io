## About
This page contains summaries of research papers I read and found interesting. 
Most will be in the field of **software systems**, specifically Operating Systems 
(especially in virtualization and security), Computer Networking, Database 
Systems, Distributed Systems, and Computer Architecture. Note that I may forget 
to list papers I've read. Also, I will not include a paper summary for papers I 
read for class assignments due to academic integrity reasons.

Most summaries will highlight the key concepts in the paper, as well as my own 
thoughts. These summaries are mainly for personal use, i.e. to consolidate my 
own understanding of each paper and summarize my own thoughts.  I hope others 
can use this page to find interesting papers to check out, or to quickly pick 
up key concepts from papers in an easily-digestible format.
