## About
This page contains a description of the courses I am/was a teaching assistant (TA) 
for, including a brief overview of the course and my responsibilities.

### F25 Distributed Systems (15-440/640)

I TA-ed for Distributed Systems (15-440/640) during Fall 2025 semester. 
Distributed Systems is one of the core courses in CMU's undergraduate/graduate-level 
software systems offerings and covers a wide range of topics, including:

- Networking (IP, UDP, TCP, Addressing, Routing, DNS, CDN)
- Clock Synchronization (NTP, Lamport Clocks, Vector Clocks)
- Distributed Mutex (Totally-Ordered Multicast, Lamport Mutex, Ricart-Agrawala Mutex)
- Distributed Replication (SMR, FLP, Paxos, Raft)
- Distributed Transactions (ACID, 2PL, 2PC)
- Fault Tolerance (Chandy-Lamport Snapshot, Error-Correcting Codes, RAID)
- RPCs
- Filesystems (NFS, AFS, GFS, HDFS)
- Cluster Computing (MPI, Actor Model, MapReduce, Apache Spark)
- Publish-Subscribe (Apache Kafka)
- Virtualization (Hypervisor, Containers, Scaling)
- Cryptography (OTP, Block Cipher, MAC, RSA, Diffie-Hellman, PKI, TLS)
- Byzantine Fault Tolerance (Dolev-Strong Protocol, PBFT)
- Blockchain (Longest-Chain Consensus, Proof-of-Work)

My responsibilities include:

- Holding **recitations** for over 40 students where I reinforce lecture content 
and introduce course projects.
- **Weekly office hours** where I explain distributed systems concepts and provide 
debugging support to students of all backgrounds (both undergraduate and graduate).
- Designing **homework and exam questions** to reinforce/test knowledge on course topics.
- **Weekly meetings** with course instructors to provide feedback from students, 
organize course logistics, and discuss course-related policies.
